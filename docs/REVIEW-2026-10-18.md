# Hydro: playtest and visual review, 18 October 2026

A fresh-eyes review of `main` at 9a60fb2, played through Playwright
screenshots in this container (SwiftShader, roughly 5× slower than a desktop).
It covers the first visit on a desktop (1280×800) and on a phone (Pixel 7),
every tool used with mouse and with touch (CDP touch events), seed 1 (256²)
watched hands-off for six years, seed 3 (128², cheat) for the power chain,
and the panels (achievements, ideas, history, tool cards, menu).

Screenshots are in `out/review/` (gitignored), with the scripts that took
them in `out/review/s/`. They are referenced below as `out/review/<name>.png`.
Severity: **P1** is a bug or a broken promise, **P2** is a readability or
feedback gap a new player will hit, **P3** is polish.

## 1. Bugs and glitches

1. **P1: the phone top bar reflows when the date gets longer.** At
   "Summer, day 2" the date panel is wider than at "Spring, day 1". The
   hamburger menu wraps to a second row on the left, under the date, and the
   speed buttons slide right. Every season or day-count change can make the
   HUD jump. Fix: give the date a fixed or minimum width, or let the menu
   button shrink before it wraps. Compare
   `out/review/10-phones3-30-wheel-sites.png` with
   `out/review/10-phones3-01-raise-selected.png`.
2. **P1: the welcome panel shows an empty light-blue box on a slow phone.**
   While the live scene loads, the art box has the class `live`, which hides
   the SVG drawing, and stays without `live-on` for more than 28 s. The
   player sees a blank rectangle on the very first screen. Keep the drawing
   visible until the canvas has actually faded in (`live-on`).
   `out/review/01-intro-phone-p1.png`. On desktop it was live after 3 s:
   `out/review/01-intro-desk-p1-early.png`.
3. **P1: the intro's last page says these are "coming later": herders and
   woodcutters, sluices, pumps and mills, hydro power with lit cottages.**
   All of them are in the game now. The note makes the game look older than
   it is, so drop or rewrite it. `out/review/01-intro-desk-p3.png`.
4. **P2: snow is blocky and grey.** In winter the snow cover shows
   cell-sized light and dark squares, like a nearest-filtered low-resolution
   mask, at every zoom. It also reads as grey fog rather than snow: trees,
   roofs and fields carry no white at all. `out/review/crop-y1-winter-snow.png`,
   `out/review/51-y1-winter-vh28.png`. In year 3 at −4 °C there is no snow at
   all, which may be correct weather but looks inconsistent:
   `out/review/51-y3-winter-vh28.png`.
5. **P2: shallow water over fields and dug cells draws as hard-edged
   rectangles.** Flooded fields and the puddle in a fresh dig show square,
   pixelated blue-grey tiles instead of a smooth shoreline.
   `out/review/51-y1-spring-d4-vh28.png` (flooded first fields),
   `out/review/10-desk-14-weir-built-3d.png` (top right),
   `out/review/10-desk-08-even-after.png` (puddle).
6. **P2: a weir built into a spring flood stays unfinished for days.** In the
   seed-1 run a 1.5 m weir was 95 % built on day 5 and still showed an orange
   plan diamond on one mid-river cell on day 10. Nothing tells the player that
   the crew cannot finish it, or why.
   `out/review/10-desk-15-weir-selected-handles.png`,
   `out/review/10-desk-20-turbine-built.png`.
7. **P2: a turbine can be accepted with "0.4 m of head: about 5.6 kW now",
   then run at 0 kW.** In the seed-3 run a gate in the same weir drained the
   pool, so the turbine was built with flow 0 and power 0. The turbine and
   its panel give no sign of this (see 3.2).
   `out/review/10-desks3-20-turbine-built.png`.
8. **P3: power poles stay after their turbine is removed.** After brushing
   Remove over the weir, the turbine, gate and pump were taken down, but the
   line of poles stayed and now leads nowhere.
   `out/review/10-desks3-43-removed.png`.
9. **P3: the population bar can show progress towards a tool that is already
   unlocked.** The bar fills from `population`, while unlocks follow `peak`.
   When the village shrinks from 15 to 14, the bar reads "almost at Weir"
   although the Weir is unlocked, and the 1.2 s width transition makes it
   jump visibly. `out/review/51-y2-summer-vh28.png`.
10. **P3: the phone keeps a gold focus ring on the last tool tapped.** After
    Escape returns to Look, the Weir button keeps its ring and looks
    selected too. `out/review/70-card-power-phone.png`.

## 2. Readability

1. **P2: the stats bar is icons only.** Two bars, four faces and two numbers
   carry no words. Only the tooltip (which phones never show) says the blue
   bar means "progress to the next tool". A new player can't tell what to aim
   for. Suggestion: a tiny "Weir at 15" caption under the blue bar, or show
   the next tool's icon at its right end. `out/review/02-first-view-desk.png`.
2. **P2: Raise, Dig and Even look the same at normal zoom.** All three
   leave a brown "loose earth" patch. Whether it is a mound, a hole or a
   levelled area can only be read from the shading at close zoom, and Undo of
   an Even stroke is nearly invisible. Ideas: a light height tint (warm for
   raised, cool for dug) for a few seconds after a stroke, and contour lines
   inside the brush ring. `out/review/10-desk-04-raise-after.png`,
   `out/review/10-desk-06-dig-after.png`, `out/review/10-desk-08-even-after.png`,
   `out/review/10-desk-09-after-undo.png`.
3. **P2: the middle of a weir disappears under the water.** In flow, the
   weir reads as two cobbled pads on the banks, with a faint ghost in
   between. There is no visible crest line, step or white spill, so the
   player cannot see the wall they built. Once the head is real (2.5 m), it
   looks great: `out/review/60-weir-turbine-close.png`. Low weirs:
   `out/review/10-desk-14-weir-built-3d.png`.
4. **P2: the water wheel's gold rings are too small and thin.** At the
   default zoom they are hairline arcs that are easy to miss. They are the
   only guide for where a wheel can go.
   `out/review/10-desks3-31-wheel-hover.png`,
   `out/review/10-phones3-30-wheel-sites.png`.
5. **P2: on a phone the hint takes three lines and stays up.** With the
   options row, the bottom 25 % of the screen is UI while a tool is held.
   Consider shortening the hint after the first use, or collapsing it to its
   ⓘ. `out/review/10-phones3-01-raise-selected.png`.
6. **P3: shallow flood water is a milky turquoise-green with grass and
   flowers poking through.** It reads as a swamp rather than as water over a
   meadow. `out/review/10-desk-15-weir-selected-handles.png`.
7. **P3: villagers' bank lining looks like random paving.** Grey cobbled
   squares sit in the middle of the fields and next to the village as a big
   plaza. It is not clear that they are lined banks or anything purposeful.
   `out/review/51-y6-summer-vh28.png`, `out/review/10-desks3-24-poles-built.png`.
8. **P3: the mini palette in the intro scene on a phone has labels that
   touch.** "LookRaise" runs together, and Even is missing from it.
   `out/review/01-intro-phone-p2.png`.

## 3. Missing feedback and confusing interactions

1. **P2: one-shot tools drop back to Look, even on a failed tap.** After a
   power tap that placed nothing (seed 1, a submerged 1.5 m weir), the tool
   silently reverted to Look and no hint was visible 0.8 s later.
   Choosing Power line or Pump means picking Power again every time.
   Suggestion: stay in the tool when nothing was placed, as the wheel
   already does, and keep the power mode selected.
   `out/review/10-desk-19-turbine-placed.png`.
2. **P2: a selected turbine says only "Turbine" and Remove.** It doesn't
   show head, flow, kW or how many houses are lit. That is the number the
   player tuned the weir for. The stats bar doesn't show power either.
   `out/review/61-turbine-selected.png`.
3. **P2: lit cottages are never seen.** The renderer is always daytime by
   design (`renderer.ts:369`), so the turbine card's promise ("the cottages
   around it light up") and the Bright Windows achievement have no visible
   payoff in the world. At least glow the windows of powered houses, or warm
   them at dusk. `out/review/60-night.png` was taken at time of day 0.85
   and is full daylight. Compare the card:
   `out/review/70-card-turbine-phone.png`.
4. **P2: drawing a power line shows no preview.** While dragging, no poles
   or line appear, unlike the pump's diamonds and the weir's ghost. The line
   only shows once the crew has built it.
   `out/review/10-desks3-22-poles-drawing.png` (compare
   `out/review/10-desks3-26-pump-drawing.png`).
5. **P2: a wheel dropped off a gold ring gives no sign.** The hint says
   "the red ghost said why", but with a drag that started on a non-site no
   ghost appeared at all. `out/review/10-desks3-32-wheel-drawing.png`.
6. **P2: the gate has no hover ghost.** Hovering a weir with the Gate tool
   shows nothing until you click. `out/review/10-desk-16b-gate-hover.png`.
7. **P2: brushes bury fields without a word.** Raise and Dig went straight
   over the villagers' young fields (and into a flood). There was no warning
   and no field-loss note, while weirs explicitly avoid houses.
   `out/review/10-desk-03-raise-holding.png`.
8. **P2: prosperity collapsed from 96 % to 12 % in years 5 and 6 of a
   hands-off run (seed 1), with nothing in the HUD to say so.** The bar just
   turns orange. The history chart shows the slide but not its cause. The
   "Ideas for this valley" entry (summer drought, dig a channel) is the right
   answer, but it hides behind the menu's news dot. Consider surfacing the top
   idea when prosperity drops sharply. `out/review/51-y6-summer-vh28.png`,
   `out/review/51-history-hover.png`, `out/review/51-achievements.png`.
9. **P3: the first view has no goal.** The village is a speck in a wide
   green plain, and after the intro nothing says "try digging a channel to
   the fields". A one-time nudge (an idea or a pulsing tool) would help in
   the first minute. `out/review/02-first-view-desk-30s.png`.
10. **P3: the phone's first camera shows the world's edge (bottom left).**
    `out/review/02-first-view-phone-30s.png`.
11. **P3: the "Show me" idea ring at a wide zoom circles the whole village,**
    which says little about where to dig. `out/review/51-idea-shown.png`.
12. **P3: no undo confirmation.** Pressing Undo gives no toast or flash.
    `out/review/10-desk-09-after-undo.png`.

## 4. Looks off

- Winter keeps leafy trees fully green (autumn had orange):
  `out/review/51-y1-winter-vh28.png`.
- The intro's background is a flat blue-grey with no world behind it,
  whereas tool cards blur the live game. It looks unfinished:
  `out/review/01-intro-desk-p1.png` against `out/review/70-card-weir-desk.png`.
- The flow streaks on the river are very dense and bright at the default
  zoom and dominate the frame: `out/review/02-first-view-desk-30s.png`.
- Houses pack wall to wall with no lanes between them (charming up close,
  but it reads as one block from afar): `out/review/crop-y3-village.png`.

## 5. Delightful: keep

- The village up close: big-headed villagers, pumpkins, wheat rows, sheep,
  the well with a roof, chimney smoke. `out/review/crop-y3-village.png`,
  `out/review/51-y6-summer-vh18.png`.
- The floating-island overview with fog clouds at the edge, and autumn's
  khaki and orange palette. `out/review/51-y3-summer-vh160.png`,
  `out/review/51-y1-autumn-vh28.png`.
- The tall weir with its turbine house and wires sagging off to the village.
  `out/review/60-weir-turbine-close.png`.
- The weir selection: handles, a dashed crest line, a clear −/+ panel, and
  touch targets that are a good size on a phone.
  `out/review/10-phones3-15-weir-selected-handles.png`.
- The history chart: log villagers with tool-unlock stars on the axis,
  season bands, rain, flood and achievement markers, and the hover readout.
  `out/review/51-history-hover.png`.
- The tool cards, with live scenes and short, purposeful text.
  `out/review/70-card-weir-desk.png`, `out/review/70-card-turbine-phone.png`.
- The brush cursors that follow the terrain (gold for Raise, blue for Dig,
  green for Even), and the Remove brush's orange highlight.
  `out/review/10-desk-02-raise-hover.png`, `out/review/10-desks3-41-remove-drawing.png`.
- The palette fits on one row on a Pixel 7, with readable labels and clear
  lock badges. `out/review/02-first-view-phone-30s.png`.
- The spring flood visibly drowning the first floodplain fields: the land
  posing a problem, as GAMEPLAY.md wants. `out/review/51-y1-spring-d4-vh28.png`.
