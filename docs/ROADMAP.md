# Hydro — Roadmap

Each milestone ends with a playable deploy to GitHub Pages and a feedback
round with the player. Status: ☐ todo · ◐ in progress · ☑ done.

## M0 — Foundations ☑
- ☑ Repo scaffold: Vite + TS + three.js, vitest, eslint, Playwright
- ☑ CI: check + test + build + e2e, deploy to Pages
- ☑ Asset pipeline: curated Kenney GLBs in `public/assets`, CREDITS
- ☑ README screenshots generated by CI and published with Pages
- ☑ Docs: DESIGN, ARCHITECTURE, TESTING, ROADMAP, CLAUDE.md

## M1a — First splash ☑
Fun on screen fast.
- ☑ 256² world: seeded terrain, macro river network carved into it,
  boundary inflow/outlets
- ☑ Local-inertial water with Manning friction; conservation + dam-break tests
- ☑ One drifting rain front at a time, soil bucket infiltration, evaporation
- ☑ Worker loop + snapshot protocol (buffer pool, skip-when-busy)
- ☑ Texture-driven terrain & water rendering, sun, camera (mouse + touch)
- ☑ Trench & dike tools, plan preview, spirit-crew builder
- ☑ Speed controls, minimal HUD
- ☑ Atmosphere: instanced Kenney trees & rocks; a few wandering
  mini-characters and cube pets (no AI yet)
- ☑ e2e smoke + interaction tests, README screenshots, deploy

## M4 — Structures ◐
- ☑ M4a Lining (no erosion/slumping), weirs (hold to the crest, spill
  above), sluice gates (hold a reservoir closed, drain to the sill open;
  tap to toggle — since M3¾ worked by the villagers), built by the spirit crew; plans only on known land;
  tools unlock at 30/50 villagers (since M3¾: 15/30); digit-key hotkeys
- ☑ Weir vs dike made plain (playtest feedback): overtopped earthen banks
  scour and breach (`CellFlag.Fill`, `hydro/erosion.ts` overtopStep), weirs
  are lined stone crests drawn as a line, shape from the crest height;
  select and edit what you built in place (`ui/selection.ts`: tap, drag a
  weir's ends along its line and its crest handle, move a pump's outlet or
  a wheel's trough, × to remove; a turbine's, pole's or pump's live power);
  earthworks take up fields and structures in
  their way and go round houses
- ☑ Player lining tool removed again: lining is left to the villagers near
  their homes (elsewhere natural erosion); `?cheat=1` unlocks all tools
- ☑ Villagers line banks by their homes and the player's canals near the
  village on their own; quays by the houses; a fishing jetty per village
- ☑ Water wheel (noria) tool at 50 villagers (GAMEPLAY §1.3,
  `sim/structures/wheel.ts`): lift from the current, trough to the bank,
  Uphill achievement, tool card scene
- ☑ Wheel rework (playtest: "banks too high"): lift up to 10 m with a
  wheel as tall as its lift, a 6-cell aqueduct to the end of the stroke,
  the quickest water (race below a weir) counts; gold rings mark good
  sites while the tool is held, a ghost wheel (red when bad) instead of
  text; `tools/wheel-sites.ts` surveys sites per seed
- ☑ Villager wells (water table within 4 m; water carriers until then;
  dry wells) and mills at head sites (the wheel turns with the flow, a
  miller, flour) — GAMEPLAY §1.1–1.2, `sim/people/wells.ts`, `mills.ts`;
  achievements Wellspring and Miller's Head
- Pumps (manual/animal): cosmetic only, not planned
- ◐ NPCs build their own small works: ☑ field ditches off the player's canals (playtest round 2); little dams, paths

## M5 — Human impact ☐
- Ploughing/sealing → runoff; overgrazing/logging → erosion; wells →
  groundwater drawdown; flood memory for house placement
- Shelved by the player ("keep it all positive"): irrigation → salinity,
  sewage → pollution downstream

## Sculpting ☑
The interaction rework decided with the owner (DESIGN §4).
- ☑ Raise / Dig / Even brushes replace Trench / Dike / Level: immediate,
  rate × hold time, soft falloff, three sizes, sticky, work while paused;
  client prediction with the sim as the final word
- ☑ Water conserved under the brush; sculpted earth loose (Fill, Canal);
  houses, jetties and structures masked smoothly; fields cleared;
  footbridges not masked: ground moved > 0.1 m at their banks or piles
  breaks them (rebuilt by the villagers as after a flood)
- ☒ Undo of the last strokes: removed (owner decision 2026-10-20: it does
  not fit the game; earthworks are for keeps)
- ☑ Stamp brushes (2026-10-20): Raise and Dig press a flattened sphere into
  (up from under) the ground at a steady rate, the slopes around relaxing
  to loose earth's angle of repose; round bowls, mounds and channels, no
  single-cell spikes; sizes 2, 3, 4.5 cells
- ☑ Brushes read apart (review 2026-10-18): own cursor shapes (Raise arrows
  up, Dig dashed with arrows down, Even a flat ring at its level), contour
  lines under the brush, the stroke's ground tinted warm (raised) or cool
  (dug) for a while
- ☑ Map-wide faint contour lines (0.5 m, 2.5 m stronger) while an earth
  brush is selected, on beds under water too
- ☑ Fields survive gentle brushing and `shapeLine`; lost only where the
  ground gets too steep to farm (faint red tint near the limit, no note)
- ☑ Every tool stays selected (after placing, or a tap that placed
  nothing) until Escape or Look; ghosts before placing: pumps, a gate on
  hover, the weir's wall while drawn and while
  built, a red wheel off its sites (no live text with any preview: the
  ghost says it); bigger, pulsing wheel-site rings
- ☑ Weir as a stone wall (≤ 4 m above its lower side, no earthworks); a
  gate drawn across a channel builds a wall with the gate
- ☑ Old saves' earthwork plans finished
  on load; bots, scenes and tests shape lines with `shapeLine` (earth moved
  counted for a later budget)
