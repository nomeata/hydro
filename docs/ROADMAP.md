# Hydro — Roadmap

Each milestone ends with a playable deploy to GitHub Pages and a feedback
round with the player. Status: ☐ todo · ◐ in progress · ☑ done.

## M0 — Foundations ☑
- ☑ Repo scaffold: Vite + TS + three.js, vitest, eslint, Playwright
- ☑ CI: check + test + build + e2e, deploy to Pages
- ☑ Asset pipeline: curated Kenney GLBs in `public/assets`, CREDITS
- ☑ README screenshots generated by CI and published with Pages
- ☑ Docs: DESIGN, ARCHITECTURE, TESTING, ROADMAP, CLAUDE.md

## M1a — First splash ☑
Fun on screen fast.
- ☑ 256² world: seeded terrain, macro river network carved into it,
  boundary inflow/outlets
- ☑ Local-inertial water with Manning friction; conservation + dam-break tests
- ☑ One drifting rain front at a time, soil bucket infiltration, evaporation
- ☑ Worker loop + snapshot protocol (buffer pool, skip-when-busy)
- ☑ Texture-driven terrain & water rendering, sun, camera (mouse + touch)
- ☑ Trench & dike tools, plan preview, spirit-crew builder
- ☑ Speed controls, minimal HUD
- ☑ Atmosphere: instanced Kenney trees & rocks; a few wandering
  mini-characters and cube pets (no AI yet)
- ☑ e2e smoke + interaction tests, README screenshots, deploy

## M1a feedback round ☑
- ☑ No day/night; seasons drive light and hue
- ☑ Tools return to Look after one stroke
- ☑ Flow direction visible (drifting foam particles, flow streaks)
- ☑ Plans visible under water
- ☑ Rain fronts visible as clouds/rain

## M1b — The land moves ☑
- ☑ Erosion/deposition/talus with safeguards, silt fertility tint; tuned to be
  stationary (sediment in ≈ sediment out, rivers keep meandering)
- ☑ Seasonal colour palette (light, sky, grass, water, leaves)
- ☑ Level tool
- ☑ IndexedDB autosave/resume, export/import save files, menu
- ☑ Long-horizon geomorphology test (stationarity; `tools/longrun.ts`)

## M1c — Deep water ◐
- ☑ Lateral groundwater (hourly Darcy diffusion), springs/bogs via overflow
- ☑ Deep percolation sink so closed basins don't fill up over years
- Snow + melt, climate anomaly (wet/dry years)
- ☑ Phone bench page (`?bench=1`)
- ☑ CPU cap on touch devices (sim duty cycle 45%, `?duty=` overrides)
- Quality tiers (pending real-device numbers)

## M3 — The people arrive ☐
See docs/PEOPLE.md (phases §10).
- Prerequisite: lateral groundwater (M1c) so canals water nearby fields
- ☑ M3-α First village: founding family, builders replace spirit crew,
  houses, farmers, fields/gardens, granary props, footbridges, baked-
  animation characters
- ◐ M3-β ☑ fishers (fish stock per calm-water block), ☑ flood flight,
  ☑ flood memory, ☑ flooded houses become ruins (rebuilt once forgotten);
  ☐ fish-jump visuals
- ☑ M3-γ Fog of war (revealed land grows with villages, cloud-sea
  rendering, plans only on known land, camera bounds), more settlements
  founded on attractive land, tool unlocks by population
- ☑ M3-δ More life: living vegetation (daily growth and decline, forest
  spreads from its edges, feedback on infiltration and erosion, stationary
  over decades), woodcutters with a woodpile, herds (cows, sheep, pigs,
  chicks) led to pasture by herders who bring home milk, desire paths;
  achievements Cattle Country, Well Trodden, Forest Keeper
- ☑ Welcome panels illustrated by live scenes from the real engine
  (staged scene world, scripted timelines, SVG fallback)
- ☑ Hidden economy made visible (PEOPLE.md §7): crop stress on fields
  (dry / waterlogged), per-village mood with chimney smoke, laundry,
  flowers and children playing; listless villagers by the store in hard
  times; per-village store with sacks, hay and a fish-drying rack

## M3½ — Endorphins ☑
Player feedback: more direct progress hits; keep it all positive
(DESIGN §4a).
- ☑ Stats bar: population (log bar between unlock milestones, sparkle at a
  milestone) and prosperity (population-weighted wellbeing)
- ☑ Daily history recorded in the sim and saved (thinned: daily for two
  years, half-seasons before, years after 20 years); `history` message
- ☑ History chart panel: villagers (log) and prosperity, season bands,
  rain, dry spells, floods, milestone stars
- ☑ Achievements per world (docs/ACHIEVEMENTS.md): checked by the sim
  against a start-of-world baseline, saved; toasts, Achievements card in
  the menu, medals on the history chart

## M4 — Structures ◐
- ☑ M4a Lining (no erosion/slumping), weirs (hold to the crest, spill
  above), sluice gates (hold a reservoir closed, drain to the sill open;
  tap to toggle), built by the spirit crew; plans only on known land;
  tools unlock at 30/50 villagers; digit-key hotkeys
- ☑ Player lining tool removed again: lining is left to the villagers near
  their homes (elsewhere natural erosion); `?cheat=1` unlocks all tools
- ☑ Villagers line banks by their homes and the player's canals near the
  village on their own; quays by the houses; a fishing jetty per village
- Wells, pumps (manual/animal), water wheels and mills
- NPCs build their own small works (irrigation ditches, little dams, paths)

## M5 — Human impact ☐
- Ploughing/sealing → runoff; overgrazing/logging → erosion; wells →
  groundwater drawdown; flood memory for house placement
- Shelved by the player ("keep it all positive"): irrigation → salinity,
  sewage → pollution downstream

## M6 — Power ☐
- Dams + turbines (head × flow), power poles with supply radius, lit houses,
  electric pumps, demand curve by day/season, pumped storage
- Power-dependent professions and higher population caps

## M7 — Endless world ☐
- Active-region growth with macro catchment model for boundary inflows
- Performance: tile sleeping, possibly WASM/GPU kernels

## Later / ideas

- Sound (Settlers-like work sounds, ambience), music
- Multiple biomes (desert oases, alpine)
